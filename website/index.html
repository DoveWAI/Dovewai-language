Technology

One platform. Many environments.

DoveWAI is being designed as a flexible foundation rather than a single model or hardware stack.

Dove Platform

A control layer intended to coordinate AI workloads, tools, permissions and execution across local and connected environments.

Adaptive Compute

A vendor-neutral direction for using available CPU, GPU, NPU and future accelerator resources instead of assuming one hardware ecosystem.

Dove Studio

A practical interface direction for people and teams to use DoveWAI capabilities without managing every underlying component themselves.

Why DoveWAI

Technology should work for people.

Our engineering direction is guided by a few durable principles.

Local when it matters.Keep useful AI close to the user and device where practical.
Privacy by design.Minimise unnecessary data movement and make deployment choices clear.
Adapt, don’t exclude.Scale from modest hardware to powerful systems instead of assuming one class of machine.
Open to ecosystems.Work across vendors, providers and local runtimes rather than forcing one dependency.

About

Australian technology with a long-term view.

DoveWAI is an Australian technology project focused on making capable AI more practical, private and adaptable across real-world computing environments. Our direction spans local AI, adaptive computing, developer tooling and future intelligent systems, with responsible engineering and measurable capability at the centre.
